The Quiet Gallery

Wren the courier has one night to cross the Vesper Gallery, lift the ledger pages, and slip out the service exit — unseen.

Guards patrol with lantern cones. Crates block their sight, bushes hide you, and the dim shadow floor halves how far they can see. Get spotted and the alarm chases you down.

  • WASD or arrow keys — move (hold Shift to sneak, slower and harder to spot)
  • Click the floor — walk to that spot
  • Esc or P — pause · R — restart after being caught · M — mute

Paused

The gallery waits. The guards keep their rounds in your head.

Caught

Out unseen

Ghost means no alarm and every page. Shadow means no alarm. Spotted means the gallery knows you were there.
